Updated 4 November 2026 · THIMRAL (PTY) LTD · South Africa

Learning progress and analytical work

DataLeek runs locally and has no DataLeek login, learning platform account, analytics or telemetry. Exercise attempts, submitted code/queries, feedback, progress, projects, datasets, models, reports, workflows and run evidence are stored in the local data folder. They are not automatically uploaded to us. You manage access, backups and retention. Learning progress is included in workspace backups; sharing project/evidence files can disclose your data.

Device activation

The app reads a stable OS/platform identifier locally and hashes it using a product-specific context. It does not use your name, hostname, files, IP address or MAC address to form the device code. Raw identifiers remain local. The app does not send an activation request automatically.

If you choose to send activation-request.json to obtain or transfer a licence, the seller receives the hashed device code, OS family and major product version along with your order reference and contact information. A device code linked to an order can be personal information. It is used to issue a device-bound licence, handle transfers and maintain necessary licensing records. The signed licence stores your purchaser name and device code locally; it does not contain the seller’s private signing key.

Credentials and external services

API keys and database passwords use the system keychain when available. If it is unavailable, local database fallback is disclosed in Settings; secure-storage policy can prohibit fallback. Do not put secrets inside free-form code, SQL, lesson answers or workflow definitions because these can be saved or exported. We do not receive your analytical credentials.

Database queries and imports go to the sources you configure. Optional update checks contact GitHub. Optional AI sends requests to your chosen endpoint and uses your own credentials and billing. The learning path and checks require no AI provider, account or API key.

Optional AI disclosure

Your typed requests and column names are sent in all AI modes. Metadata mode withholds values and prior assistant outputs. Aggregate mode adds numeric summaries. Source-value mode permits values, result tables, samples, earlier code and errors. Policy can cap disclosure. A request preview shows the dataset context; information you type is itself part of the request. A “local” model at a remote configured address receives traffic at that address.

Website, purchases and licence delivery

This static site has no trackers or advertising cookies. Hosting and downloads use GitHub, which processes technical connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. PayFast processes payment under its privacy policy. We do not receive card details.

The seller uses necessary purchaser/contact information, transaction references and activation codes to verify payment, issue/transfer licences, provide support and meet lawful accounting and record-retention obligations. This does not create a DataLeek account or transfer your analytical datasets. Unnecessary information should be removed when no longer required, subject to applicable legal retention.

Support and your rights

Review support material before sending it. Do not post activation requests, licence files, personal purchase details, credentials or source data in public issues. Use the private contact on your receipt for activation and order matters. Under POPIA and other applicable laws, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to lawful retention duties. General product reports can use the support repository.
