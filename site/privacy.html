The short version

DataLeek runs on your computer. We don't collect, receive or store your data, and the app has no account, analytics or telemetry.

What stays on your computer

Datasets, analyses, notebooks, reports, models, settings, backups and the audit log are stored in DataLeek's folder on your computer. API keys and database passwords are kept in your system keychain. You can erase everything from Settings → Data & privacy.

When DataLeek uses the internet

Only when you ask it to, or for update checks:

  • Update checks (once a day, can be turned off): the app asks GitHub for the latest version. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website visit.
  • Online imports from a web address, Kaggle, Hugging Face or OpenML: the request goes to that site.
  • Database connections you set up: queries go to your database server.
  • Cloud AI providers you choose (for example Claude or ChatGPT) with your own key: column names, summary statistics and, only if you allow it, five sample rows are sent to that provider under your agreement with them. With a local model (Ollama or LM Studio) nothing leaves your computer.

This website and downloads

This page and the downloads are hosted by GitHub, which processes visitors' IP addresses and similar technical data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page sets no cookies and has no trackers.

Support requests

If you open an issue or send us a diagnostics file, we use what you share only to help you. Diagnostics files contain the app log and version details, not your datasets, keys or code. Please don't post private data in public issues.

Your rights and contact

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws, you may ask what personal information we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. Contact us by opening an issue at github.com/Thimral/DataLeek-releases.
